Dungeon Handbook

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details on gameplay mechanics, v2.2.3 release scope, platforms, and community links.

What makes Backpack Ascendancy different from standard auto-battlers?

Every weapon cooldown, quiver volley, glyph status rider, armor link, and Catalyst Stone auto-trigger depends strictly on how you rotate and neighbor items on your dungeon grid before and during combat.

How much content is included in version 2.2.3?

Version 2.2.3 features 250 pixel-art items across 24 families, +15 gear forging and merges, 203 Ascendancy stars, 239 enemies, 4 heroes (Warrior, Rogue, Mage, Summoner), 6 biomes, Endless Mode, Weekly Challenges, Ancient Trials, Corrupted Nodes, offline play, and 2-player online co-op in 7 languages.

Where can I play Backpack Ascendancy right now?

Backpack Ascendancy is playable right now on Android via Google Play and on PC via Steam, with the iOS App Store release coming soon.

Where can I share builds and follow updates?

Share #build and #tree planner URLs, check full patch notes in the Developer Updates chronicle above, and join players directly on the official Backpack Ascendancy Discord server.
