Game support

Need help or found a bug? Email ioniuncu@gmail.com.

Please include your device model, operating system, game version, what happened and the steps that led to it. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords or account recovery codes.

Progress and saves

Core gameplay and local saves work without a gamer account and offline. Removing the app can remove local progress.

Optional online backup uses Google Play Games on Android or iCloud on iOS. Use the same platform account when returning to the game. On iOS, iCloud and iCloud Drive must be enabled. Saves do not transfer between Android and iOS.

Achievements and leaderboards

Online game services need an internet connection and Google Play Games on Android or Game Center on iOS. Core gameplay remains available if you dismiss platform sign-in.

Purchases and restoring the Supporter Pack

The Supporter Pack is a single, non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. It removes in-game ads, adds 500 crystals once, starts every run with a Catalyst Stone, increases run crystals by 50%, unlocks Endless Ascendancy, and includes an instant revive on the first death of a regular run. Endless runs do not include that revive.

On iOS, use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Open the Supporter Pack panel from the main menu and select Restore. On Android, ownership is checked through your Google Play account. Restoring does not grant the 500 crystals again. Purchases do not transfer between Apple and Google accounts.

Your store shows the local price and any applicable taxes before purchase. Apple or Google handles payment, cancellation, and refund requests. If a purchase is pending, allow the store to finish processing before trying again. Never email payment-card details.

Advertising and privacy choices

Without the Supporter Pack, an interstitial ad may appear after a boss battle, before the next stage map. Closing it returns you to the game. Supporter Pack owners do not receive these ads.

You can decline any rewarded ad and keep the normal reward earned through play. An ad may be unavailable because of your connection, region or ad inventory. Where required, you can revisit your ad privacy choices in Settings → Privacy & Ads.

On iOS, declining tracking permission does not block gameplay. You can change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Languages

English, French, Spanish, German, Polish and Simplified Chinese are available in the in-game settings.
