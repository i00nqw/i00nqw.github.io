Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-15 · Last updated: 2026-09-25

Summary

  • We collect gameplay analytics so we can find bugs and understand how the game is played.
  • The game offers optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. You choose whether to watch each ad in exchange for the reward shown in the game.
  • Google services may process technical data such as your IP address, IP-derived approximate location, device or app identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics.
  • Optional Google Play Games or Apple Game Center features use your gamer profile, achievements, leaderboard scores, and cloud save data.
  • The game does not request your email, phone number, or address, or access your contacts, photo library, personal files, or messages.
  • We do not collect precise or GPS location. Google's analytics service does derive an approximate, city-level location from your IP address — see "What we collect".
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not charge real money for anything in the app.
  • Core gameplay and local saves work without platform sign-in and offline. When you use platform game services, game data can also be stored online; cloud synchronization, analytics, and rewarded ads use a network connection.

What we collect

Gameplay analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to record events generated by the game. Examples include:

  • Which hero you picked
  • Which stage you reached
  • How long a run lasted
  • Whether you won or died
  • How many enemies you defeated

Firebase may also automatically process technical information such as your device model, operating system, app version, language, Firebase installation ID, IP address, and an approximate location derived from your IP address.

Rewarded ads

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to offer optional rewarded ads. When the SDK initializes or an ad is requested, displayed, or watched, Google may automatically collect or process:

  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location
  • Device and app identifiers, including the Android advertising ID when it is available under your device settings
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, video views, and ads viewed
  • Diagnostic and performance information related to the app and the ad SDK

This data is used to deliver and measure ads, provide analytics, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain service security. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. The app does not request precise or GPS location.

Platform game services and cloud saves

On Android, the game uses Google Play Games Services; on iOS, it uses Apple Game Center. These features are optional for core gameplay. The platform may sign you in automatically according to your account settings.

When signed in, the app receives your platform gamer ID, display name, and avatar when available. It reports achievement progress and leaderboard scores, including run details such as stage, node, and gold earned. Your gamer profile, scores, and achievements may be visible to other players according to the platform's profile and privacy settings.

The game can back up and restore your save through Google Saved Games on Android or Apple iCloud on iOS. This includes progress, unlocks, crystals, run records, and game settings. These backups are associated with your Google Play Games account on Android or iCloud account on iOS. Apple cloud saves require iCloud and iCloud Drive. Google and Apple use separate save services; they do not provide an Android-to-iOS save transfer through this game.

What we do not collect

  • Email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses through gameplay
  • Precise or GPS location
  • Contacts, photo-library images, personal videos or files, or calendar entries; platform avatars and game-created save data are described above
  • Messages of any kind (the app has no chat, forums, or user-to-user interaction)
  • Payment or financial information; the app has no purchases or payment flow
  • Health or fitness data
  • Web browsing history

How we use data

We use gameplay and technical information to understand how the game performs, find bugs, improve balance, and decide which features need attention. Ad-related information is used to request, display, measure, and reward optional ads and to detect invalid or fraudulent activity. Platform gamer information connects your progress to your platform account, displays your profile and rankings, and supports achievements and save backup or restoration.

Optional rewarded ads

Rewarded ads are optional. The game identifies the reward before you choose to watch, and an ad starts only after you select the relevant watch-ad button. Declining an ad does not remove the normal rewards earned through gameplay. Ad availability depends on your connection, region, device settings, and Google's ability to provide an eligible ad.

Where required, Google displays a privacy message before the app requests ads. If Google requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point in your region, you can reopen that form from Settings → Privacy & Ads.

Who receives data

We use Google Firebase Analytics for gameplay analytics and Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK for optional rewarded ads, ad measurement, and fraud prevention.

Google may process data as described in the Google Privacy Policy and Google's information about how it uses data from sites or apps that use its services. We do not sell or rent your personal data.

We also use Google Play Games Services and Saved Games for Android gamer profiles, achievements, leaderboards, and save backups, and Apple Game Center and iCloud for corresponding iOS game services and saves. Apple processes game-service and iCloud data under its Privacy Policy and Game Center privacy information.

Data storage

  • Your save file, including heroes, unlocks, crystals, and progress, is stored in local app storage and may also be backed up to Google Saved Games or Apple iCloud when you use those services. The providers retain cloud game data under their service policies and your account settings.
  • Analytics data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection and is retained under our Firebase settings and Google's applicable policies.
  • Advertising data is processed and retained by Google under its applicable service and privacy policies.

Your choices and controls

  • You may decline any rewarded ad and continue playing without that optional ad reward.
  • You can manage or delete your Android advertising ID through your device's privacy or Google Ads settings. Available menu names differ by Android version.
  • You can manage Google ad personalization through My Ad Center.
  • You can play locally without platform sign-in. Use Google Play Games or Apple Game Center settings to manage sign-in and profile visibility.
  • Google provides controls to delete Play Games data or your Play Games profile. Manage Apple service data and iCloud saves through your Apple account and device storage settings.
  • Uninstalling the app stops future collection from that installation and normally removes its local save data. It does not necessarily delete cloud saves, achievements, or leaderboard records. Reinstalling and signing in may restore cloud progress.
  • The game uses your platform gamer ID for game services and does not ask you to create a separate Backpack Ascendancy account. Contact us if you have a privacy question.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact ioniuncu@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change how the app collects or uses data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.

Contact
