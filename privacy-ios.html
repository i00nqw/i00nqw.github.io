Backpack Ascendancy Privacy Policy

For the iPhone and iPad app. Last updated: October 23, 2026.

Backpack Ascendancy is operated by Ion Iuncu. Questions or privacy requests: ioniuncu@gmail.com.

Summary

Core gameplay and local saves work offline and without a game-service account. The app uses Google Firebase Analytics for gameplay measurement, Google AdMob for ads, Apple StoreKit for the optional Supporter Pack, and Apple Game Center and iCloud for optional game services. We do not sell personal data or collect precise/GPS location, contacts, photos, messages, health information or payment-card details.

Gameplay Analytics

Google Firebase Analytics records gameplay and app events, such as hero selection, stages reached, run duration, wins and losses, equipment actions, and purchase conversion. Firebase also processes device model, operating system, app version, language, app-instance and installation identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostic information. Purchase measurement may include product identifiers, price and currency. These data help us understand usage, improve balance, investigate errors and measure app performance.

Firebase advertising storage, advertising user-data sharing and ad personalization are disabled by default. Firebase IDFV collection is disabled. Gameplay analytics remains enabled independently of optional ad tracking; declining tracking does not mean that all analytics stops. We do not send Game Center profile names or cloud save contents to Firebase Analytics.

Ads and Advertising Data

Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK provide optional rewarded ads and interstitial ads at eligible breaks after boss encounters. Rewarded ads begin only after you choose the stated reward. Interstitial ads do not provide an optional reward. Buying the Supporter Pack removes ads as described in the game.

When the advertising SDK initializes or requests, displays or measures ads, Google may process IP addresses and approximate location, app and device identifiers, advertising identifiers when permitted, app launches, taps, ad views, purchase-related measurement, crash data, performance information and other diagnostics. Purposes include delivering and measuring ads, analytics, developer advertising measurement, fraud prevention and security. Data is encrypted in transit.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents consent choices before ads are requested. When regional privacy options apply, reopen them from the game's Settings > Privacy & Ads. Availability of ads depends on consent, region, connection and eligible inventory.

Tracking Permission

The app requests Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission before allowing advertising-identifier access. With permission, Google AdMob may use the IDFA for personalized advertising and measurement across other companies' apps and websites. Denying permission does not block gameplay, earned rewards, purchases or game services. Eligible ads may still be served without IDFA, subject to your other consent choices. We do not substitute another identifier to bypass your tracking decision.

Change permission in device Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Ad consent and tracking permission are separate controls.

Purchases

Apple StoreKit processes the optional Supporter Pack purchase. The app uses product, transaction, purchase-status and entitlement information to deliver benefits and restore purchases. Firebase may measure purchase conversion. Apple handles payment; we do not receive card numbers or bank details. Apple's transaction retention and refund rules apply.

Game Center and iCloud

If you use Game Center, the app receives your gamer identifier, display name and available avatar, reports achievements and leaderboard scores, and associates those results with your profile. Scores and run details, such as stage, node and gold, may be visible to other players according to Game Center settings. Optional iCloud saved games contain gameplay progress, heroes, unlocks, inventory and settings so progress can be backed up or restored. These data support game functionality, not advertising.

Providers and Retention

Google receives the analytics and advertising data described above under its Privacy Policy and partner-services information. Apple processes purchases, Game Center and iCloud data under its Privacy Policy and Game Center privacy information. Providers may process data outside your country under their applicable safeguards and policies.

Local saves remain in app storage until removed. Cloud saves and service records are retained under your Apple account settings and Apple's policies. Analytics data is retained according to our Firebase retention settings and Google's policies; advertising records follow Google's applicable service policies. Contact us for information or requests concerning data we control. We cannot promise immediate deletion of records independently retained by providers or required for legal, security or transaction purposes.

Your Choices and Requests

  • Play locally without signing in to Game Center; manage profile visibility and service data through your Apple account and device settings.
  • Decline rewarded ads; normal earned rewards remain available. Use the Supporter Pack to remove ads.
  • Manage tracking permission and regional ad consent using the controls described above.
  • Manage iCloud saves through your Apple account and device storage settings. Uninstalling removes the installation's local data and stops future collection from it, but does not automatically erase cloud saves, achievements, leaderboard records or provider transaction data. Reinstallation may restore cloud progress.
  • Request access, correction or deletion of personal data we control, or ask about analytics choices, by emailing ioniuncu@gmail.com. We may need information to identify the relevant record and verify a request. Provider-held information is also subject to the provider's own account and privacy controls.

The game does not create a separate Backpack Ascendancy account and does not request your personal email address, telephone number or street address. Information you voluntarily send when contacting support is used to respond to your request.

Children and Policy Changes

The app is intended for a general audience, not specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided such information. Material changes to these practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
